CFD Analysis of Technology Upgrading of Cracking Furnace

Duration: 2020– 2021

Institution: Persian Gulf University

This project introduces an innovative coke recycling system for ethylene-ethane cracking furnaces. Traditional decoking requires costly 48-hour shutdowns, with extracted coke transported offsite for disposal, causing environmental pollution and high operational costs. The new patented closed-loop system uses a "coke trap" to directly reinject extracted coke back into the furnace as fuel. This approach reduces fuel consumption, eliminates waste disposal costs and environmental impact, saves space, and improves production efficiency.

Tools: ANSYS Fluent

Pumps Analyzed: P-202, P-204, P-401, P-604
