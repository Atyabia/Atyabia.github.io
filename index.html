Zn Battery Modeling

Hybrid Zn-ion/Air Battery Modeling

Duration: 2024 – Present

Institution: KU Leuven, Belgium

Developed a 1D transient COMSOL model of a hybrid zinc-ion/air battery (Zn anode | separator | Co₃O₄ cathode) following the unified data-physics framework (Maghsoudy et al., 2026). Incorporates Butler–Volmer kinetics for four reactions, dynamic porosity via Bruggeman correlation, and ML surrogates (BO-LSSVM / BO-RF) across 405 parametric scenarios for specific capacity and discharge voltage prediction.

Tools: COMSOL, Python, Scikit-Learn

Key Results: R²=0.998 (discharge voltage), R²=0.980 (Zn-ion capacity)

View Details

Vanadium Flow Battery

Vanadium Flow Battery – Ion-Selective Membrane Modeling

Duration: 2024 – Present

Institution: KU Leuven, Belgium

Developed advanced multiphysics models for vanadium redox flow batteries with a focus on ion-selective membrane transport using Poisson–Nernst–Planck (PNP) equations. The model captures coupled ionic flux, electric potential distribution, and species crossover, enabling performance prediction and membrane selectivity optimization for large-scale energy storage.

Tools: COMSOL Multiphysics, Python

Key Results: PNP equations, membrane ion selectivity, scale-up
