Innovative Coke Recycling System

Closed-Loop Solution for Ethylene-Ethane Cracking Furnaces

Project Overview

This project introduces an innovative coke recycling system for ethylene-ethane cracking furnaces. Traditional decoking requires costly 48-hour shutdowns, with extracted coke transported offsite for disposal, causing environmental pollution and high operational costs. The new patented closed-loop system uses a "coke trap" to directly reinject extracted coke back into the furnace as fuel.

Challenges with Traditional Decoking

During cracking operations, coke forms on the inner surface of furnace coils, requiring periodic removal. Traditional decoking methods involve extracting coke particles using increased steam and air injection, followed by collection through cyclone filters. This process presents several significant challenges:

Key Problems

  • Extended Downtime: Average 48-hour shutdown per decoking cycle, reducing production capacity
  • High Operational Costs: Collection, transportation, storage, and disposal expenses
  • Environmental Impact: Offsite incineration or burial creates pollution and regulatory challenges
  • Equipment Degradation: Coke particles damage downstream equipment, increasing maintenance costs
  • Space Requirements: Significant site area needed for collection and storage systems

Patented Closed-Loop Solution

The innovative system eliminates waste by treating extracted coke as a valuable fuel resource rather than a disposal problem. During decoking operations, coke is collected in a storage tank and then directly reinjected into the furnace using a specialized coke trap system.

Key Design Features

The coke trap connection to the furnace features a specially engineered junction designed to withstand high temperatures and pressures without structural failure. This closed-loop configuration ensures seamless integration between the decoking process and fuel reinjection system.

Project Benefits

  • Fuel Savings: Reduces overall fuel consumption by recycling coke as energy source
  • Zero Waste: Eliminates offsite transportation, incineration, and disposal costs
  • Environmental Protection: Significantly reduces carbon footprint and pollution
  • Space Efficiency: Compact closed-loop design requires minimal site area
  • Equipment Longevity: Reduces wear on downstream equipment
  • Production Efficiency: Maintains operational capacity by minimizing downtime impact

Impact and Results

This approach effectively reduces fuel consumption, eliminates waste disposal costs and environmental impacts, saves site space, and maintains production efficiency by recycling the coke as fuel rather than treating it as waste. The system represents a significant advancement in sustainable pyrolysis operations and demonstrates the value of circular economy principles in industrial processes.

Innovation Highlights

The patented closed-loop coke recycling system transforms a costly waste management challenge into an efficient fuel recovery process, delivering both economic and environmental benefits while maintaining operational reliability.
