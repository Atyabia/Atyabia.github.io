Rotary Piston Pump Design and Numerical Simulation

CFD Analysis and Performance Optimization for High-Pressure Seawater Pumping Applications

Project Overview

  • Target: Design, simulate, and optimize a specialized rotary piston pump for seawater pumping applications capable of handling high pressures up to 35 bar.
  • Approach: Utilized a reverse engineering methodology to calculate fluid chamber volumes and rotational speeds based on flow rates and volumetric efficiencies. Implemented a systematic two-phase CFD validation framework in ANSYS Fluent benchmarked against established lobe pump configurations.
  • Achievements: Mapped precise internal flow patterns, velocity vectors, and pressure cycles across three distinct lobe geometries (sinusoidal, circular, and involute profiles) to establish guidelines for preventing internal leakage.
